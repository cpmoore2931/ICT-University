## ICT Episode 8 — Applying Institutional Order Flow to Forex (short one)

**What this video is:** Proof the model isn't just for futures. Same pattern, forex pairs, daily chart included.

### The model on a DAILY chart (EURJPY example)
- Relative equal lows swept → two swing highs broken (structure turns up) → fair value gap in the up leg → price returns to the gap → launches toward the old high.
- **The draw is a horseshoe post.** You aim at it. Whether the shoe rings the post doesn't matter — the aim is the trade. Enough range between entry and the draw = worth the stab.
- He hates JPY pairs (they double-back to levels) — shown only for completeness.

### Forex session times (New York local time, always)
- **New York killzone: 7:00–10:00 AM** — hunt the setup here.
- **London killzone: 2:00–5:00 AM** — same pattern, that window.
- That's it. Skip Asia and London close while learning.

### Same top-down drill
15-min: mark the leg → drop 5m → 3m → 1m. Swing low taken + swing high broken + FVG in the leg = setup. Two stacked FVGs: expect the stab into the lower one (same rule as before).

### Targets with nothing to the left (expansion moves)
- Anchor a fib to the PREVIOUS swing (low to high after the consolidation) and project **standard deviations** (e.g., -1). His projection nailed the high to the point. (Fib settings: his OTE Primer video.)

### Pair logic (simple)
Check the two parts of the pair: EUR futures (6E) going up = euro strong. Yen weak. Strong ÷ weak = EURJPY goes UP. That's the whole narrative check for crosses.

## ICT Episode 9 — Power Three + New York PM Session

**What this video is:** The daily candle's story — accumulation, manipulation, distribution — plus the "don't chase the overnight run" rule and afternoon setups.

### Power Three (the shape of every daily candle)
Bullish day:
1. **Open** near the low
2. **Manipulation** — a fake dip BELOW the open (the **Judas swing**) — this is where smart money accumulates longs
3. **Expansion/rally**
4. **Close** near the high (distribution — smart money hands off their longs)

Bearish day = mirror image (open, fake pump up, dump, close near low).
Never try to predict the CLOSE. Just anticipate the fake move and which way the candle expands.

### The overnight run rule (equities only — NQ/ES/YM, not forex)
Big run happened overnight/London before you woke up?
- **Do NOT chase. Skip the NY morning session entirely.**
- Wait for after 1:00 PM. Expect the morning/lunch lows (relative equal lows where overnight longs trail their stops) to get swept → then the afternoon rally continues the move.
- After a big run there's usually consolidation. Choppy consolidation = the blowtorch that burns accounts. Wait.

### High-probability order block (recipe recap)
Bullish narrative + displacement up + the consecutive down-close candles right before the surge + an imbalance sitting with it → anticipate the return into it. FVG + order block + OTE stacking together = strong zone.

### The 2 PM afternoon trade he walked through (1-min chart)
- Post-lunch: relative equal lows swept (sell side taken) → swing high broken WITH energy → FVG left behind → price dips back in → long.
- Entry: limit at the short-term low (or a tick inside the FVG). Stop: below the low that formed after the FVG.
- Example math: ~14 handles risk, ~3.5R to the target. On 6 micros: risk ~$85 to make ~$300.
- **A wick poking through the FVG does NOT invalidate it.** Watch the candle BODIES respect the level. In violent conditions, allow more imperfection and let the stop do its job.
- Missed the first entry? The pattern often re-offers (second FVG, order block below).

### Small clock notes
- 2:00 PM New York = when the afternoon setup window really comes alive.
- He treats **4:30 PM** as his practical "close" checkpoint for the day's range.

## ICT Episode 10 — Economic Calendar Events & The Open

**What this video is:** Why 8:30 matters, THE daily-bias lesson (his honest full answer), and the opening range concept.

### The economic calendar
- Check **forexfactory.com** (or any calendar) EVERY day before trading. Red = high impact, orange = medium.
- **8:30 AM = the news embargo lifts.** That's the real reason 8:30 is the magic time.
- Pre-news tell: if bias is bearish and price PUMPS UP ahead of the release — they're pricing in a premium so the news can be the excuse to dump it. The shorts were accumulated before the headline.

### Daily bias — his complete honest answer
- Bias = **will today's candle expand HIGHER or LOWER than its OPENING price?** That's it. Not the close. Not the exact high/low.
- Read the daily chart with Power Three glasses: bearish leg = candles open, pop up a little, close low. Count the up-close candles in a down leg — there are barely any (he counted 2, then 5 in a big leg).
- One red day in a bullish leg means NOTHING. Don't abandon bias until the draw (the liquidity pool) is reached.
- You WILL have loss days inside a correct bias. If a few losing days blows your account, the problem is leverage, not bias.
- A swing low/high is **3 candles.** No Williams fractal, no extra candles needed.

### The OPENING RANGE (the big new tool)
Bearish day:
1. Opening price (he uses **midnight New York open** on the daily-range view; 8:30 open also used intraday).
2. The Judas rally makes a high above the open. **Open-to-high = the opening range.**
3. ALL your short setups (FVG entries, stop raids, OTE) form between the open and that high.
4. Missed it? **Project that same range BELOW the open** — "close proximity entries" form just under the open inside that projected zone.
5. He also projected the range **×2 lower** — another short formed right at it.
6. Price leaves the projected zone? **You can't chase. Done.** Discipline or nothing.

### Top-down FVG search — the exact drill (now explicit)
Check 5-min → 4-min → 3-min → 2-min → 1-min.
**STOP at the first timeframe that shows the gap.** Found on the 4? Don't go lower. Nothing on ANY of them? **No trade.**

### Breaker (brief mention)
High → low → higher high pattern. Lets you place a TIGHTER stop (just above the breaker candle) instead of way above the whole run. Full lesson elsewhere on his channel.

### Risk sermon (write these down)
- NQ can move 100 points in ONE minute. Slippage blows through stops.
- Discount-broker margins ($1.5–2k/mini) = asking to blow up. He wants **$10–15k minimum per NQ mini.** Micros while learning.
- **When the exchange RAISES margins, that's them telegraphing: huge moves are coming.** (His note-worthy line.)
- The $10k→$58k in 2 weeks demo was PAPER trading with discount margins, shown only because people asked. Not a promise. Not the goal.
- **Traders are professional losers** — every trade opens as a loser (spread + commission). Trading = managing losing trades, turning most into winners, surviving the rest.

### Back-testing mindset
- Annotating old charts = telling yourself the story = pseudo-experience that trains your subconscious.
- Even better: screen-record the live session (Camtasia) and replay the real tape later — better than TradingView's clunky replay.
- Bias bearish? Then you're ONLY allowed bearish FVGs. Taking a bullish gap on a bearish day isn't the model failing — it's you leaving the model.
- If forced to keep ONE timeframe forever: **the 15-minute.** It's the storyline for everything.

## ICT Episode 11 — Live Trade Review + Swing High Classification (Tuesday, pre-market-structure lesson)

**What this video is:** He replays a real 246-handle NQ short he called beforehand, and introduces HOW to rank swing highs (short-term / intermediate / long-term) — the warm-up for Thursday's market structure lecture.

### The trade, start to finish
1. Daily chart: price rebalanced up into the daily FVG (premium), spent 3 days consolidating in it. The whole time, the called draw = sell stops below the old daily low (his green line, marked in advance).
2. Bearish bias → morning rally after 8:30 = Judas swing. He wants shorts ABOVE the 8:30 open.
3. Entry 10:33 AM at the TOP of a 5-min imbalance: 13,858.25. Wicks poked outside the zone — he ignores wicks, watches **bodies**.
4. Displacement came → confirmation → "sit back and let it do its thing."
5. Monday holiday: futures close 1 PM, reopen 6 PM NY. It had stopped right ON the level — he expected a gap down. Reopen: gapped down and accelerated. Covered at 13,612.25. **~246 handles.**
6. After the run into the low: take profits, step aside. The gap left behind then gets rebalanced (it did).

### Clock clarification (people were confused)
- **Midnight NY open** → reference for the WHOLE daily range (daily Power Three).
- **8:30 AM open** → reference for the MORNING session.
Two different tools for two different windows.

### Ranking swing highs (from Larry Williams, early '90s)
Every swing high = 3 candles (lower high on each side). But they NEST:
- **Short-term high** = any 3-candle swing high. (Old-school mark: one halo ⭘)
- **Intermediate-term high** = a swing high with LOWER swing highs on both sides of it. (Two halos)
- **Long-term high** = an intermediate high with lower intermediate highs around it. (Three halos)

Why it matters: he shorted near a swing high that sat UNDER an intermediate-term high — structure says that high should NOT be taken while the draw is below. So the "chop" wasn't noise; each lower swing high was the market telling him the short was safe. This is much more than "lower highs, lower lows."

### Missed the first entry? The re-entry rule again
After displacement breaks the low, price returning to the fresh imbalance = your second chance. Same FVG entry technique. He'd rather be positioned NEAR the session high (optimal) than chase a breakout below the low.

### After a big win — protect the win
- The dopamine hit after a real win makes you want to jump right back in. DON'T. He goes to a demo account on purpose to scratch the itch without burning the live win.
- "Push your edge" is gambler talk. **You dull an edge by pushing it. You sharpen it by knowing when to get in, when to get out, and when to sit.**
- After his big move: next morning = no live trades (also the day-after-big-move rule: skip the morning).

- Book recommendation: Larry Williams, *How I Made One Million Dollars Trading Commodities* — the market structure chapters are still gold (skip the moon phases).
- Broker statements can show weird negative "adjustments" at settlement — accounting, not a losing trade.
- Holiday schedule: close 1 PM, reopen 6 PM NY.
