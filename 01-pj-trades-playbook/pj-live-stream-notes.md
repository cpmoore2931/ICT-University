## The layer PJ adds ON TOP of ICT
PJ runs the ICT model (sweep → V-shape/recovery → inversion → draw) but blends in:
- **VWAP family**: session VWAP, Friday's VWAP, Sunday VWAP, NY AM VWAP — treated as dynamic magnets/rejection spots. "Come up, kiss the VWAP, fail above it."
- **Volume profile**: Point of Control (POC — daily, weekly, monthly), Value Area High/Low (VAH/VAL). Old POCs/VAH/VALs = interest levels. Price "flipping" or "holding" a POC matters.
- **Auction Market Theory (AMT)**: big imbalance move → lands in a high-volume "balance" area → chop while buyers/sellers agree on value → NEXT imbalance move leaves FROM the chop. Chop = balance, not noise to trade.
- **CVD (cumulative volume delta)**: heavy negative delta = rallies are suspect; a real flip needs volume behind it.
- **Order book / "the book"**: watches resting interest at levels; "the book isn't friendly for higher."
- **Gains (co-host)** = the volume/orderflow voice; PJ = the PD-array/structure voice. They cross-confirm.

## PJ live vocabulary (new/confirmed)
- **The box / Lizard Lounge levels** — pre-marked zones of interest (e.g., "135–150 box"). Everything between boxes is NOISE.
- **IFVG / inversion ("IFEG", "continuation inversion")** — gap flips role; continuation inversions print on slow bleed days.
- **Recovery** — THE direction tell. Out of a zone, a real V-shape recovery is fast and snappy with follow-through. Slow, lethargic recovery = don't trust the long. "It's always the recovery, bro."
- **Macros** — :50 to :10 windows (10:50, 11:10, 11:50 mentioned). Moves often start/end inside them.
- **The wedgy** — trapped between a bullish gap below and bearish gap above = guaranteed chop. Stand aside.
- **No man's land** — a setup printing far from a real level (not off Asia high, not off a pool) = skip even if it works.
- **LRLR** — low resistance liquidity runs ("so much LRLR to the downside").
- **Run vs sweep, relative equals, three-drive, SMT, displacement** — all used exactly as the 2022 model taught.
- **"Trump tweet low"** — a news-spike low becomes a legit liquidity pool/draw afterward.
- **Base hits**: 25-point trades. His prop math is built entirely on them.

## The workflow shown live
1. Pre-market: mark the boxes (HTF gaps, old POC/VAH/VAL, session VWAPs, London/Asia levels). Name draws BOTH directions.
2. Wait for price to enter a box. In between = watch, talk, do nothing.
3. In the box: demand the REACTION (snappy recovery/displacement + a printable setup — 15m internal-to-external, inversion, gap entry).
4. No reaction or no printable setup = no trade, even when the draw call was RIGHT. He called 210 and the 130–150 box; both hit; he took nothing — "I know where it's going, I just don't have a way of trading it."
5. Chart hygiene: **mark up the continuous contracts NQ1! and ES1! ("big daddy"), execute on MNQ or whatever.** Micros wick differently — never mark up on MNQ. Pay for real-time data.
