# ORB Morning Checklist — v1

For Page's 8:00 AM Gold ORB. Full model lives in `claude/page-orb-strategy.md`.

**How to use this:** every box is yes or no. No "kind of." No "looks like."
**If any box is NO, there is no trade.** That's the whole point — you are training
a rule, not a feeling.

Anything marked **DRAFT** is a starting number to test, not a law. Trade it,
watch it, change it. Then it becomes yours.

## PART 4 — the entry gate

All four must be yes.

- [ ] 1. Low was swept
- [ ] 2. Price came back and is **holding above the ORB mid** ✅ condition 2
- [ ] 3. Previous high above is **still untagged**
- [ ] 4. It is inside the trade window (~9:30–10:40)

Any NO → stand down.

### Where you get in — DRAFT

Entry is a **price**, not a moment. Pick it before price gets there.

- The leg that reclaims the mid leaves a **1-minute gap** behind it.
- **Entry = the pullback into that gap, or the retest of the mid.**
- If price never comes back — you missed it. That is a fine outcome. Say it out
  loud: *"I missed it, and that's fine."*

**Entry price = ____________**

### Stop — DRAFT

One tick below the sweep low, **or** below the entry gap — whichever is closer.

**Stop = ____________**

## PART 5 — the math box (5 seconds, every time)

Fill this in **before** you click. Not after.

| | Points |
|---|---|
| Entry to stop (risk) | |
| Entry to target (reward) | |
| **Reward ÷ risk** | |

**If reward is less than 2× risk → DO NOT TAKE IT.**

Doesn't matter how good it looks. Doesn't matter that you waited all morning.
The math says no, so it's no. This one line is what stops a chase.
